## Batch 4: animated themes (generated and original art)

No third-party images. The raster backgrounds were generated for these themes with the `codex` CLI's
built-in image generation (prompts asked for original scenes with no people, text, logos or brands),
then resized and re-encoded as WebP with metadata stripped.
